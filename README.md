
# Chronofracture

Chronofracture is 2D Sidescroller Roguelike action game where each death isn't the end of your run, but an oppurtunity to make it better. Dying in Chronofracture sends you across the multiverse into a new dimension, each each with their own unique abilities and weapons you can acquire to use on the rest of your run.

Getting to round 10 gets you into a Final boss fight with Loki, God of Time and Stories, and King of the Multiverse.

Chronofracture has 3 distinct dimensions you can visit, each with their own unique abilities and weapons you can aquire:

  ## Arcane:
  Following a future machine uprising, humanity rid the world of technology and relied on ancient arts of magic and arcana to provide the backbone for civilization and human advancement

  - **Blink:** C teleports you to the cursor
  - **Nova:** Periodic blast around you
  - **Seeker Bolts:** Bolts home in on enemies
  - **Soul Leech:** +3 health per kill
  - **Spell Book:** Hold F or right mouse to charge, release a piercing beam
  - **Frost Imbue:** Weapon hits slow enemies
  - **Fire Imbue:** Weapon hits set enemies burning
  - **Shock Imbue:** Weapon hits arc to nearby enemies
  - **Toxin Imbue:** Weapon hits make enemies take more damage

  ## Medieval:
  A future where humanity failed to evolve past the Dark Ages and has been stuck in a stagnant state of advancement and has been overun by evil monsters

  - **Sword:** Auto-slash toward the cursor; later picks offer other weapon forms
  - **Spear:** Melee form: long, narrow thrust
  - **Axe:** Melee form: wide, heavy cleave
  - **Flail:** Melee form: hits all around you
  - **Daggers:** Melee form: short and very fast
  - **Crossbow:** Slow, heavy bolts · Lv2 split on impact · Lv3 explode
  - **Orbiting Blade:** +1 sword circling you
  - **Tower Shield:** +40 max health and a full heal
  - **Plate Armor:** -20% damage taken
  - **Whetstone:** +35% damage

  ## Cyberpunk:
  A dystopian future where humans are now more man than machine. The Cyberpunk universe has many technological implants to offer, augmenting your run to include cutting age tech

  - **Twin Blaster:** +1 bolt per shot
  - **Overclock:** +30% fire rate
  - **Thrusters:** +20% move speed
  - **Rail Rounds:** Bolts pierce +1 enemy
  - **Sandevistan:** SHIFT slows time, longer per level
  - **Arm Cannon:** R fires an explosive rocket at the cursor
  - **Grenades:** Q lobs a grenade at the cursor
  - **Neural Hack:** E turns the enemy at the cursor on its allies (slows Loki)
